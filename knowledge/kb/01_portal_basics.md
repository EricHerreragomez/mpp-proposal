## Standard self-service flow
1. Go to My Product Portal
2. Open the product
3. Click Edit (pencil icon next to product name)
4. Make the change
5. Review all tabs for pending updates
6. Submit

## Default escalation
If the user can't edit (no permissions, inactive PM/TL, ownership problem, portal data issue):
"Scroll to the bottom of the Product page and click *Tell us about wrong data!* — the MPP Team will assist."

## What MPP is
The Unified Product Catalog / My Product Portal is the system of record for core product metadata:
Product ID, ownership (PM/TL/Sponsor), lifecycle status, Charge Code, classification fields, security/compliance metadata.
Product attestation is required every 3 months to keep it accurate.
Note: an MPP Team data update does not mean the product's attestation is complete.

## Integrations
- Finance (FCT / SAP): Charge Code → Cost Center Department and Cell
- ServiceNow CMDB: tagged cloud resources sync within ~24 hours
- AVM: cloud account ownership / Product ID for AVM accounts
- Launch / PIX / Engage / SAP-CPM360: governance, publishing, pricing flows
- GHD Software Catalog: register in MPP first, then request addition to GHD catalog
