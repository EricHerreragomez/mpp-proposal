# Service Offering & Support Routing

## Hierarchy
Business Application → Application Service → Business Service → Service Offering → Assignment Group
Also: Technical Service → Technical Service Offering (infrastructure layer).
Service Offering routes support (incident assignment tiers).

## Level 2 support / Assignment Group
Not editable on the product record (shows read-only with a green check when the Service Offering is validated).
1. MPP → product → Service Offering section
2. Open the Service Offering record
3. Update the Assignment Group
4. Save/Submit
PM/TL only. CMDB sync ~24h. Still stuck → services_readiness@mckinsey.com.

## Creating a Service Offering
Create the Service under Product Services first; then map it when creating the Service Offering. Service exists but "Delivered Service" dropdown is empty → services_readiness@mckinsey.com.

## Multi-module products
Option A: single product, multiple Service Offerings (IRP covers highest classification).
Option B: separate Product IDs if modules are independent.
